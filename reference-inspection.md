# Slice AI Source Inspection Notes

## Homepage — Initial Desktop Observation

The source homepage uses a light cool-gray canvas with a near-black/charcoal text color. The top navigation is centered within a wide bounded content area: a compact `slice_ai` wordmark at the left, a rounded white central navigation capsule, and a bordered `Contact Us` button on the right. The hero is an oversized, softly rounded rectangle over a faint structural grid. It contains a small capsule eyebrow, a large centered dark headline, short supporting text, a pink-orange-purple gradient primary pill, a white secondary pill, and a large tilted, frosted product-dashboard visual that overlaps below the hero.

The observed homepage content sequence is: header; hero; five-item feature ticker; Main Features; four audience/use-case cards; AI Powered Assistant statistics; a secondary assistant CTA; trusted-client logo strip; compatibility/product visual; About Us figures and testimonial; FAQ; article cards; final CTA; newsletter/footer.
