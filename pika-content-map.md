# Pika Website Messaging Map

The supplied master description is the sole content authority. The site will position Pika as a **Discord intelligence workspace**, never as a generic analytics product, scraper, Discord replacement, or unsupported source of claims.

| Existing surface | Pika message role |
|---|---|
| Global header and footer | Pika intelligence workspace; **Contact us** in the navigation; **Get started** as the primary action. |
| Homepage hero | “Discord is full of signals. Pika helps you find the ones that matter.” |
| Homepage workflow | Discover → Monitor → Search → Detect → Understand → Act. |
| Homepage sections | From noise to intelligence; Ask Pika; intelligence rather than information overload; the bigger idea; final CTA. |
| Features and feature detail pages | The six workflow stages as connected capabilities, with concise explanations based strictly on the master description. |
| About and use-case content | Built for founders, freelancers and developers, sales and growth teams, agencies and consultants, researchers and analysts, and community operators. |
| Pricing and calls to action | No invented plan benefits, customer proof, statistics, or promises. Use “Try Pika Free”, “See how Pika works”, and “Get started”. |
| FAQ | Explain Pika’s relationship to Discord, its signal-over-noise approach, the workflow, and who it serves. |
| Blog direction | Community intelligence, opportunity discovery, monitoring, search, trend detection, and conversation-led research. |
| Supporting pages | Remove all lorem ipsum and template language; use focused, credible copy consistent with Pika’s positioning. |
