# Pika Product-Clarity Audit

## Current issues to remove

The current copy overuses abstract terms such as “intelligence,” “signals,” “context,” “discover,” and “what matters.” It explains a framework before it shows a job the product can do. Several route headings describe an idea rather than a practical outcome.

## Rewrite rules

Every prominent heading must say what a visitor can find, watch, search, or do. Supporting text should give a short concrete example. Discord should appear where it adds useful context, not as the subject of every section.

## Homepage clarity sequence

1. **What Pika is:** Find useful conversations, people, communities, and opportunities online.
2. **What it finds:** Developer requests, topic-specific communities, discussions about a problem, and changing conversations.
3. **Why it helps:** Search across many conversations without digging through them; get notified when something important happens.
4. **Who it helps:** Founders, developers, teams, consultants, researchers, and community operators.
5. **What to do next:** Search, save, monitor, and follow up from one place.

## Product-led visual direction

Replace decorative or generic artwork in major explanatory sections with visual mockups showing search results, conversation results, people seeking help, community discovery, monitoring alerts, saved items, filters, and a concise context panel. The product UI should carry the explanation; copy should stay brief.
